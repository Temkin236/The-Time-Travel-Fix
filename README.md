# ⏳ The Time Travel Fix

**Challenge #01 – CSEC Social Media Team (2025)**

A simple AI-powered project built with **Google AI Studio** and **me** to redesign old CSEC posters into clean, modern 2025 visuals — without changing the original person or text.

---

## 📌 What is this project?

**The Time Travel Fix** is a small but powerful tool that takes an old or messy poster and gives it a **fresh, professional look**.

The goal is not to change history — but to **present it better**.

---

## 🎯 Main Objectives

* Modernize old CSEC posters
* Improve readability and layout
* Keep the **same text information**
* Keep the **same person and real face**
* Make posters ready for social media in 2025

---

## 🔐 Important Rule (Face Safety)

* The person’s face is **never changed**
* No AI face generation or replacement
* No filters that change identity
* Only light improvements (brightness, contrast)

👉 The person stays **real, natural, and authentic**

---

## ✨ What the AI Improves

* Layout and spacing
* Font clarity and hierarchy
* Color balance
* Background simplicity
* Overall professional look

---

## 🧠 Division-Based Styling

The design style adapts based on CSEC divisions:

* **Development** – Clean, grid-based tech look
* **Cyber** – Dark, secure, minimal style
* **Data Science** – Data and network-inspired visuals
* **Capacity** – Clear, growth-focused design
* **Events** – Dynamic and eye-catching layout

---

## 🛠 Tools Used

* Google AI Studio
* AI image understanding
* Prompt-based design rules

---

## 🔄 How It Works (Simple Flow)

1. Upload an old poster ("Before")
2. AI analyzes layout, text, and visuals
3. AI redesigns the poster
4. Output a clean 2025 version ("After")

---

## 📥 Input & Output

**Input:**

* Screenshot or image of an old CSEC poster

**Output:**

* Clean, modern poster
* Same content, better design
